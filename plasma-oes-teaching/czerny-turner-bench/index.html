    Formulas · grating geometry
    Grating equation
    \[ m\lambda = d\,(\sin\alpha + \sin\beta),\quad d = 1/G \]
    Czerny–Turner sine drive
    \[ \begin{gathered} m\lambda = 2d\cos\tfrac{D_v}{2}\,\sin\phi \\ \alpha=\phi+\tfrac{D_v}{2},\quad \beta=\phi-\tfrac{D_v}{2} \end{gathered} \]
    Angular dispersion
    \[ \frac{d\beta}{d\lambda} = \frac{m}{d\cos\beta} \]
    Slit magnification (dispersion plane)
    \[ \mathcal{M} = \frac{f_2\cos\alpha}{f_1\cos\beta} \]
    Grating resolving power
    \[ R = mN = mG\,W,\quad W = \frac{f/F}{\cos\alpha} \]
    Formulas · dispersion on the detector
    Reciprocal linear dispersion
    \[ \mathrm{RLD} = \frac{d\lambda}{dx} = \frac{d\cos\beta}{m f_2} \]
    Wavelength at detector position x
    \[ \begin{gathered} \lambda(x) = \frac{d}{m}\big[\sin\alpha + \sin\beta(x)\big] \\ \beta(x) = \beta + \arctan\frac{x}{f_2} \end{gathered} \]
    Per pixel
    \[ \delta\lambda_{\text{pix}} = \mathrm{RLD}\times p \]
    Light collected (étendue)
    \[ G_{\text{ét}} \approx w\,h\,\frac{\pi}{4F^2} \propto \frac{w}{F^2} \]

    Resolution budget

    Rule of thumb: slit, 2-pixel sampling and diffraction in quadrature. The close-up below shows the actual instrument shape (slit ⊗ one pixel), which is narrower.
    Formulas · resolution
    Slit image
    \[ \Delta\lambda_{\text{slit}} = w\,\mathcal{M}\,\mathrm{RLD} \]
    Pixel sampling (Nyquist, 2 px)
    \[ \Delta\lambda_{\text{pix}} = 2p\,\mathrm{RLD} \]
    Diffraction limit
    \[ \Delta\lambda_{\text{diff}} = \frac{\lambda}{mN} \]
    Quadrature sum
    \[ \begin{gathered} \Delta\lambda_{\text{res}}^2 \approx \Delta\lambda_{\text{slit}}^2+\Delta\lambda_{\text{pix}}^2 \\ {}+\Delta\lambda_{\text{diff}}^2 \end{gathered} \]

    Formulas · line shape and plasma parameters
    What the CCD records
    \[ \begin{gathered} I_{\text{rec}}(\lambda) = \big(I_{\text{true}} \otimes g_{\text{IF}}\big)(\lambda) \\ g_{\text{IF}} = \Pi_{\text{slit}}\otimes\Pi_{\text{pix}}\otimes g_{\text{diff}} \end{gathered} \]
    Doppler FWHM
    \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{\Delta\lambda_D}{\lambda_0} = \sqrt{\frac{8k_BT\ln 2}{Mc^2}} \\ \approx 7.16\times10^{-7}\sqrt{\frac{T\,[\mathrm{K}]}{M\,[\mathrm{amu}]}} \end{gathered} \]
    Stark FWHM (Gigosos, González & Cardeñoso 2003 fits; Hβ fit to be checked against the original)
    \[ \begin{gathered} \Delta\lambda_S^{\mathrm{H}\beta} = 4.8\,\mathrm{nm}\left(\frac{n_e}{10^{23}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}}\right)^{0.68116} \\ \Delta\lambda_S^{\mathrm{H}\alpha} = 1.098\,\mathrm{nm}\left(\frac{n_e}{10^{23}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}}\right)^{0.67965} \end{gathered} \]
    Voigt width (Olivero & Longbothum 1977)
    \[ \begin{gathered} \Delta\lambda_V \approx 0.5346\,\Delta\lambda_L \\ {}+ \sqrt{0.2166\,\Delta\lambda_L^2 + \Delta\lambda_G^2} \end{gathered} \]
    Removing the instrument (Gaussian approximation)
    \[ \begin{gathered} \Delta\lambda_D = \sqrt{\Delta\lambda_{\text{rec}}^2 - \Delta\lambda_{\text{IF}}^2} \\ T\,[\mathrm{K}] = M\left(\frac{\Delta\lambda_D}{7.16\times10^{-7}\lambda_0}\right)^2 \end{gathered} \]

    Experiments to try

    1. Resolve the Hg yellow doublet. Switch to the Hg–Ar lamp, press the 578 nm preset, and set 300 g/mm. Raise the groove density until 576.96 and 579.07 nm separate. Then open the slit to 150 µm and watch them merge again.
    2. Find the pixel limit. At 1800 g/mm, close the slit from 100 µm to 5 µm. The budget bar stops shrinking once the slit term falls below the pixel term. Swap to 13.5 µm pixels and repeat.
    3. Measure an ion temperature. Select Ar II 480.60 nm in the plasma. Lower the temperature until the verdict says the Doppler width is below the instrument limit. Note that temperature, then find a setup (focal length, grating, order) that brings it back.
    4. Stark density from Hβ. Pick Hβ and sweep the electron density from 10¹⁹ to 10²³ m⁻³. Compare the recovered density with the true value. Where does the instrument start to bias the answer?
    5. Higher order. At 486 nm with 1200 g/mm, compare m = 1 and m = 2. Dispersion doubles, but the reachable range halves; try 2400 g/mm in second order and explain the warning.
