Plasma diagnostics · optical emission spectroscopy

Plasma Spectroscopy Teaching Tools

Two browser applications for a course on spectrometers and optical emission spectroscopy of argon plasmas. Nothing to install: open a tool and it runs in your browser. Your data files never leave your computer.

Czerny–Turner Bench

Explore how a grating spectrometer works.

  • 3D model, cut-away and to-scale ray geometry
  • Grating equation, dispersion and resolution with live numbers
  • Simulated Ar–H plasma and Hg–Ar lamp spectra
  • Doppler and Stark line-width exercises

Plasma OES Analyzer

Analyse your own triplicate OES measurements.

  • Background subtraction, mean ± SD
  • Peak identification (Ar I, H, O, OH, N₂, N₂⁺)
  • Ar I Boltzmann plot with LTE checks
  • N₂ rotational (gas) temperature
  • Electron density from Ar I 696.5 nm broadening

The Czerny–Turner Bench loads three.js and MathJax from public CDNs, so it needs internet access. The OES Analyzer is fully self-contained and also works offline once downloaded. See the README for data-format details and the scientific assumptions behind each analysis.
