Probe Sheath Ion Lab

Ions drift in from the plasma toward a negatively biased cylindrical Langmuir probe, seen end-on. Switch between the Laframboise (orbiting ions), ABR (radial ions) and collisional pictures and watch how many ions the probe actually collects.

Qualitative model for teaching: the potential profile is prescribed (log-shaped sheath, thickness from the Impedans manual Eq. 8), not solved self-consistently, and the presets use a high Ti/Te so that orbits are easy to see. Use the doc's curves for numbers.
